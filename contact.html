FAQ

Common Questions.

How quickly do you respond?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us — most messages get a reply within a few hours during business days.

Which countries do you serve?

We serve residential clients worldwide, with a focus on Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Central Asia. All communication is in English via WhatsApp and email. We handle international shipping and customs documentation.

Do I need to visit China to work with you?

Not at all. Most of our clients work with us entirely remotely — via WhatsApp, email, and video calls. We share detailed photos, videos, and 3D renderings at every stage. If you do wish to visit, we welcome you to our Foshan showrooms and partner factories by appointment.

What information should I include in my inquiry?

To help us respond effectively, please include: your project location, property size (m²), project stage (planning / construction / furnishing), what you need (furniture / materials / design / full solution), budget range, and timeline. Floor plans or reference images are always helpful.

What is the typical project timeline?

It depends on scope. Initial concept takes 5–7 business days after commitment deposit. Custom furniture production typically takes 6–10 weeks, plus international shipping (4–8 weeks by sea). Full-home projects with design support may take 3–6 months from start to delivery.

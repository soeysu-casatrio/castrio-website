Frequently Asked Questions

What does the commitment deposit include?
Your deposit secures our team for your project and includes a First Concept Package — a 2D soft furnishing plan and a photorealistic 3D rendering of one main space, plus space planning, styling direction, and preliminary product selections. Delivered in 5–7 business days with 1 complimentary revision. The deposit is 100% credited toward your final 30% balance payment.
What happens to my deposit?
Every dollar of your deposit is fully credited towards your final 30% balance payment. It is an advance, not an extra cost. Once concept work has commenced, the deposit is applied to your project and remains fully credited toward your final order.
Is the commitment deposit the same as the design fee or production deposit?
No, they are separate:

Commitment Deposit: Secures your project and includes the First Concept Package (2D plan + 3D rendering). 100% credited toward your final 30% balance payment.

Design Fee: For full interior design service (if needed). Paid in full before the full design process begins. Separate from the commitment deposit.

Production Deposit (70%): Paid after you approve the final product list and quotation, to begin manufacturing. Separate from the commitment deposit.
What if I'm not satisfied with the initial concept?
After receiving your commitment deposit, we schedule a dedicated discovery call or video consultation where our designer communicates directly with you to fully understand your vision, preferences, and requirements before beginning the initial concept. You also receive 1 complimentary revision to refine the direction. If you require additional revisions beyond the first, we can discuss an additional revision package based on the scope of changes.
How long does it take to receive the First Concept Package?
After the discovery call and requirement confirmation, the First Concept Package is typically delivered within 5–7 business days. This includes the 2D soft furnishing plan, 3D rendering, space planning, styling direction, and preliminary product selections for one main space (default: living room).
Is the commitment deposit refundable?
The commitment deposit is 100% credited toward your final 30% balance payment — it's an investment in your project, not a separate fee. Once we commence work on your First Concept Package (after the discovery call), the deposit is applied to your project. If you decide not to proceed before we begin any concept work, please contact us to discuss your situation. We evaluate refund requests on a case-by-case basis, but our goal is always to find a solution that works for both parties.

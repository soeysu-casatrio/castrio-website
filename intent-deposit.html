Frequently Asked Questions

What does the intent deposit cover?

It confirms your project and allows our team to begin project assessment, sourcing research, and preparation of a preliminary proposal with styling plan, space planning and product direction.

Is the intent deposit refundable?

The deposit is non-refundable once work has commenced, but it is 100% credited toward your procurement order. If you change your mind within 24 hours before we start work, a full refund is available.

How do I choose the right package?

Select based on your interior floor area and whether you need building materials. Your CASA TRIO contact will confirm the correct package with you before payment. Projects over 500㎡ are quoted individually.

What happens after payment?

Within 24 hours of receiving your deposit, we will confirm your project details and begin the assessment and sourcing process. You will receive regular updates via WhatsApp or email.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept international bank transfer (T/T) and online payment via Airwallex and WorldFirst. Your CASA TRIO contact will provide the specific payment details for your package.

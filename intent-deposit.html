Frequently Asked Questions

What does the commitment deposit include?
Your deposit secures our team for your project and includes a First Concept Package — a 2D soft furnishing plan and a photorealistic 3D rendering of one main space, plus space planning, styling direction, and preliminary product selections. Delivered in 5–7 business days with 1 complimentary revision. The deposit is 100% credited toward your final product payment.
What happens to my deposit?
The commitment deposit is 100% credited toward your final product payment. It secures our team and covers the initial concept work — your First Concept Package. Once concept work has commenced, the deposit is applied to your project and remains fully credited toward your final order.
How do I choose between "Furniture Only" and "Including Materials"?
Choose "Furniture & Decor Only" if you already have your building materials and finishes sorted, or only need help with furniture and soft furnishings. Choose "Including Building Materials" if you need us to source tiles, flooring, cabinetry, bathroom fixtures, and other hard finishes as well.
What happens after I pay the deposit?
After payment, you'll be connected directly with our project team via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a discovery call to understand your needs, then begin working on your First Concept Package. Typical turnaround is 5–7 business days.
Can I upgrade my tier later?
Absolutely. If your project scope expands, you can upgrade to a higher tier at any time. The difference will be added to your deposit and remains fully credited toward your final order.
Is the commitment deposit the same as the design fee or production deposit?
No — they are three separate payments.

Commitment Deposit: Secures your project and includes the First Concept Package (2D plan + 3D rendering). It is 100% credited toward your final product order.

Design Fee: Charged separately only if you require full interior design services (complete 3D renderings, VR, construction drawings). The design fee is independent of the commitment deposit and follows its own payment schedule (50% to start, 50% upon design direction approval).

Production Deposit: Paid after you approve the final product list and quotation, to begin manufacturing. This is separate from the commitment deposit and is applied to your product balance. Paying the commitment deposit does not automatically start production — production begins only after the final quotation is confirmed and the production deposit is received.
What if I'm not satisfied with the initial concept?
After receiving your commitment deposit, we schedule a dedicated discovery call or video consultation where our designer communicates directly with you to fully understand your vision, preferences, and requirements before beginning the initial concept. This ensures the first proposal aligns with your expectations. You also receive 1 complimentary revision to refine the direction. The commitment deposit is 100% credited toward your final product order — it's an investment in your project, not a separate fee.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer (T/T), PayPal, and Western Union for commitment deposits. For larger orders, we primarily use bank transfer. Payment details and instructions will be provided after you select your tier and connect with our team. All payments are securely processed and we'll send you a confirmation receipt once payment is received.
Can I pay in my local currency?
Our prices are listed in USD for consistency across all international clients. However, you can pay in your local currency — the amount will be converted at the prevailing exchange rate on the day of payment. We'll confirm the exact amount in your currency before you make the payment, so there are no surprises.
How long does it take to receive the First Concept Package?
After your discovery call and payment confirmation, we typically deliver your First Concept Package within 5–7 business days. This includes the 2D soft furnishing plan, 3D rendering of one main space, space planning, and styling direction. For larger or more complex projects, we'll provide a specific timeline during your discovery call. You'll receive regular updates throughout the process.
What if I already have my own designer or design?
That's completely fine! If you already have a designer or completed design, we can work directly from your drawings and provide furniture and material procurement services only. In this case, the commitment deposit still applies — it secures our sourcing team and includes preliminary product selections and budget planning based on your existing design. You won't be charged a separate design fee since you're providing the design.
Is the commitment deposit refundable?
The commitment deposit is 100% credited toward your final product order — it's an advance payment for your project, not a separate fee. Once we commence work on your First Concept Package (after the discovery call), the deposit is applied to your project. If you decide not to proceed before we begin any concept work, please contact us to discuss your situation. We evaluate refund requests on a case-by-case basis, but our goal is always to find a solution that works for both parties.

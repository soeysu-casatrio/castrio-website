Frequently Asked Questions

What does the intent deposit include?
Your deposit secures our team for your project and includes an initial concept proposal with space planning, styling direction, and preliminary product selections. For clients opting for full design services, the first concept includes a 3D rendering of one space.
Is the deposit refundable?
The intent deposit is fully creditable towards your final product payment. If you decide not to proceed before we begin work on your concept, the deposit can be refunded minus any incurred costs. Once concept work has started, the deposit covers our design and planning time.
How do I choose between "Furniture Only" and "Including Materials"?
Choose "Furniture & Decor Only" if you already have your building materials and finishes sorted, or only need help with furniture and soft furnishings. Choose "Including Building Materials" if you need us to source tiles, flooring, cabinetry, bathroom fixtures, and other hard finishes as well.
What happens after I pay the deposit?
After payment, you'll be connected directly with our project manager via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a discovery call to understand your needs, then begin working on your initial concept proposal. Typical turnaround is 5-7 business days.
Can I upgrade my tier later?
Absolutely. If your project scope expands, you can upgrade to a higher tier at any time. The difference will be added to your deposit and remains fully creditable towards your final order.

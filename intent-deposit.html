Frequently Asked Questions

What does the commitment deposit include?
Your deposit secures our team for your project and includes a First Concept Package — a 2D soft furnishing plan and a photorealistic 3D rendering of one main space, plus space planning, styling direction, and preliminary product selections. Delivered in 5–7 business days with 1 complimentary revision. The deposit is 100% creditable towards your final product payment.
What happens to my deposit?
The commitment deposit is 100% creditable towards your final product payment. It secures our team and covers the initial concept work — your First Concept Package. Once concept work has commenced, the deposit is applied to your project and remains fully creditable against your final order.
How do I choose between "Furniture Only" and "Including Materials"?
Choose "Furniture & Decor Only" if you already have your building materials and finishes sorted, or only need help with furniture and soft furnishings. Choose "Including Building Materials" if you need us to source tiles, flooring, cabinetry, bathroom fixtures, and other hard finishes as well.
What happens after I pay the deposit?
After payment, you'll be connected directly with our project team via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a discovery call to understand your needs, then begin working on your First Concept Package. Typical turnaround is 5–7 business days.
Can I upgrade my tier later?
Absolutely. If your project scope expands, you can upgrade to a higher tier at any time. The difference will be added to your deposit and remains fully creditable towards your final order.

Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

Provide detailed sourcing requirements: products, quantities, budget, lead time, quality standards and target market. You must provide clear specifications — we do not offer design or requirement research.

Step 2: Deposit & Agreement

Sign the sourcing agency service agreement. Pay upfront deposit (USD 300–2,000, covers factory screening and supply chain matching). Deposit is 100% deductible from your final service fee.

Step 3: Factory Sourcing & Comparison

We shortlist 3–5 best-fit factories from our verified network. Obtain quotations, compare pricing, quality and lead times. Provide factory profiles and samples for your review. You choose the best fit — we never push a particular factory.

Step 4: Confirm Factory & Place Order

You confirm the final factory. We set up a group chat for direct factory communication. You sign the purchase contract directly with the factory. You pay product deposit directly to the factory (typically 30%).

Step 5: Production Monitoring & QC

We monitor production progress and provide regular updates. Pre-shipment quality inspection is included free of charge as part of our service — we inspect every order before it leaves the factory, with detailed QC reports, photos and videos. Defective items require factory rework or replacement. Mid-production inspections (if you require additional checks during production) are available at an extra fee of USD 200–500 per visit.

Step 6: Logistics Coordination & Delivery

We recommend and coordinate trusted logistics partners for your shipment — we work with 3–5 reliable freight forwarders, each with expertise in different shipping routes. We'll get you competitive quotes and help you choose the best option. You contract and pay the logistics company directly — we don't mark up shipping costs or act as a freight forwarder. We assist with packaging coordination, customs documentation, and shipment tracking until delivery.

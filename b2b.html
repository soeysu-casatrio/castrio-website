Our Sourcing Process

Six Steps from Inquiry to Delivery

A clear, transparent process designed to minimise risk and maximise efficiency.

1

Submit Your Requirements

Provide detailed requirements: products, quantities, budget, lead time, quality standards. You must provide clear specifications — we do not offer design or requirement research.

Output: Requirements confirmation & feasibility assessment

2

Deposit & Agreement

Sign the sourcing agency agreement. Pay upfront deposit (covers factory screening and supply chain matching). Deposit is 100% deductible from your final fee.

Output: Signed service agreement & deposit receipt

3

Factory Sourcing & Comparison

We shortlist 3–5 best-fit factories from our verified network. Obtain quotations, compare pricing, quality and lead times. Provide factory profiles and samples. You choose the best fit — we never push a particular factory.

Output: Factory comparison table, quotations, samples

4

Confirm Factory & Group Chat

You confirm the final factory. We set up a group chat for direct factory communication. You sign the purchase contract directly with the factory. You pay product deposit directly to the factory (typically 30%).

Output: Final order confirmation, group chat, factory proforma invoice

5

Production Follow-up & QC

We monitor production progress and provide regular updates. 100% quality inspection before shipment (or sampling per your request). Provide detailed QC reports, photos and videos. Defective items require factory rework or replacement.

Output: Production progress reports, QC reports, photos/videos

6

Logistics & Delivery

Arrange packaging, freight booking and customs declaration. Provide logistics options (sea/air/rail). Assist with customs docs. Track shipment until delivery.

Output: Bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, tracking

🚗

Optional: Factory Visit Concierge

Planning to visit factories? We offer factory visit accompaniment at USD 150/day, including car transfer, translation and itinerary arrangement. Client accommodation and meals self-managed. Minimum 3 days advance booking.

How It Works

Your RFQ Journey

From submission to quotation — a transparent, efficient process designed to save you time and money.

1
Within 24 Hours

RFQ Confirmation

We receive your request, assign a dedicated sourcing consultant, and send you an RFQ reference number. We'll follow up if any details need clarification.

2
3–5 Business Days

Factory Screening

We identify 3–5 matching factories from our verified supplier network, conduct initial audits, and confirm they can meet your specifications, quantity, and quality requirements.

3
5–7 Business Days

Quotation Collection

We collect detailed quotations from each factory, including unit price, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, and packaging. We verify pricing accuracy and negotiate on your behalf.

4
7–10 Business Days

Quote Delivery

You receive a comprehensive comparison report with all factory quotations, supplier profiles, our recommendations, and a clear breakdown of our sourcing commission.

5
As Needed

Samples & Factory Visits

Arrange sample shipments (costs refundable on bulk orders) or schedule factory visits. We can also conduct virtual factory tours via video call.

6
When Ready

Order Confirmation

Once you select a supplier, we finalize terms, prepare the purchase agreement, and coordinate production. Our commission is 100% deductible from the sourcing initiation fee.

Prepare Your RFQ

What You'll Need

Having these details ready helps us provide the most accurate quotations in the shortest time. Don't have everything? Submit what you have — we'll help fill in the gaps.

📐
Required

Product Specifications

Dimensions, materials, colors, finishes, and any specific quality standards or certifications required (CE, UL, FSC, etc.).

🖼️
Required

Product References

Photos, drawings, catalog pages, or links to similar products. The more visual references, the more accurate the matching.

📦
Required

Quantity & SKU Count

Estimated order quantity per item and total number of SKUs. This directly affects unit pricing and MOQ requirements.

💰
Helpful

Target Price Range

Your budget or target price per unit. This helps us identify the right tier of suppliers and avoid wasting time on mismatches.

📅
Helpful

Target Delivery Date

When you need the goods delivered. This helps us assess production lead times and recommend appropriate shipping methods.

🚢
Helpful

Destination & Incoterms

Destination port/address and preferred Incoterms (FOB, CIF, DDP, etc.). This helps us provide accurate logistics quotes.

Our Policies

Samples, MOQ & Customization

Clear policies to help you plan your sourcing strategy with confidence.

📦

Sample Policy

  • Sample Cost: Factory actual cost, refundable on bulk orders
  • Sample Lead Time: 7–15 days (standard), 15–30 days (custom)
  • Shipping: Customer pays (use your DHL/FedEx account)
  • Quantity: 1–2 pieces per item
  • Virtual Samples: Available via video call
📊

MOQ (Minimum Order)

  • Standard Furniture: 10–50 pieces per item
  • Soft Furnishings: 50–100 pieces per item
  • Building Materials: By area / weight
  • Custom Products: Negotiable based on tooling
  • Mixed Orders: Multiple items can combine to meet MOQ
🎨

Customization (OEM/ODM)

  • OEM: Your design/drawings, factory produces
  • ODM: Factory design, modify color/material/logo
  • Customizable: Size, color, material, logo, packaging, function
  • Tooling Fee: Refundable after reaching order volume threshold
  • Sampling: 15–30 days, confirm before mass production

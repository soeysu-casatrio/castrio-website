Frequently Asked Questions

What does the B2B sourcing deposit include?
Your sourcing deposit secures our sourcing team's time and covers factory screening, supplier matching, and quotation services. We will screen 3–5 best-fit factories from our verified network, compare pricing and quality, and provide detailed quotations. The deposit is 100% credited toward our service fee.
How is the sourcing deposit amount determined?
The sourcing deposit ranges from USD 300 to USD 2,000, based on your project size, product complexity, and order quantity. Small sample orders start at USD 300, while large bulk procurement projects may require up to USD 2,000. We will provide a customized quote after understanding your specific requirements.
Do I pay factories directly or through CASA TRIO?
For B2B sourcing, you pay factories directly for products. We do not handle product payments or add hidden markups. You only pay our transparent service fee (typically 2%–10% of order value, based on project size). This ensures full transparency and the best possible pricing from factories.
What happens after I pay the sourcing deposit?
After payment, you'll be connected directly with our sourcing agent via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a call to understand your product specifications, quantity, budget, and quality requirements. Then we begin factory screening and typically provide 3–5 quotations within 3–7 business days, depending on product complexity.
Is the sourcing deposit the same as the service fee?
No. The sourcing deposit is an upfront payment to secure our team's time and cover initial factory screening work. The service fee (2%–10% of order value) is our total charge for the full sourcing service, including factory matching, price negotiation, production follow-up, quality inspection, and shipping coordination. The sourcing deposit is 100% credited toward the final service fee.
What if I'm not satisfied with the factory options provided?
We include 1 round of additional factory screening if the initial options don't meet your requirements. We'll discuss your feedback and refine our search criteria. If we still cannot find suitable factories after the second round, we'll discuss whether the deposit can be partially refunded based on work already completed.
Do you offer factory visit support?
Yes! We offer factory visit accompaniment for an additional fee of USD 150 per day. This includes car transportation, translation, and coordination with factory visits. Your accommodation and meals are not included. We can also arrange factory video tours remotely if you cannot travel to China.
What payment methods do you accept for the sourcing deposit?
We accept PayPal for sourcing deposits (credit/debit cards, PayPal balance). For service fee payments, we accept bank transfer (T/T). We'll provide detailed payment instructions after confirming your project scope.
Can I pay in my local currency?
All prices are quoted in USD. PayPal will automatically convert your local currency to USD at the current exchange rate. For bank transfers, we'll provide our USD bank account details and your bank will handle the conversion.
What products can you source for B2B clients?
We source a wide range of home and furniture products, including but not limited to: upholstered furniture, solid wood furniture, custom built-in cabinetry, bathroom fixtures, tiles and flooring, lighting, home decor, outdoor furniture, and commercial furniture. If you have specific product requirements, contact us and we'll confirm availability.
Do you handle quality control and shipping for B2B orders?
Yes! Our B2B sourcing service includes production follow-up, pre-shipment quality inspection, and shipping coordination. We work with 3–5 reliable logistics partners and will provide competitive shipping quotes. You can pay the logistics company directly, or we can coordinate on your behalf. We recommend purchasing cargo insurance for all shipments.
Is the sourcing deposit refundable?
The sourcing deposit is 100% credited toward our service fee, not refunded in cash. If you decide not to proceed after factory screening work has commenced, the deposit covers the work already completed. If we cannot source suitable factories for any reason on our end, we will refund the deposit in full. Each case is evaluated individually.

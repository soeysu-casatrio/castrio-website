A complete-home project involves much more than sending a product catalogue. Our team needs to review your project, match products, communicate with factories, check specifications, calculate quantities and prepare a detailed quotation.

The commitment deposit allows us to formally start this project-level process. It is not a service fee — it is 100% creditable toward your final product order (deducted from your 30% balance payment).

Once paid, we deliver your First Concept Package within 5–7 business days, including a 2D soft furnishing layout and 3D rendering for one key space.

They are two completely separate charges:

  • Commitment Deposit — Starts your project and covers the First Concept Package (1 space 2D + 3D). It is 100% deductible from your final product order.
  • Design Fee — For a full interior design service (all spaces, complete drawings, VR tour, construction docs). Paid 100% upfront before the full design process begins.

You only pay the design fee if you need CASA TRIO to handle the full interior design. If you already have design plans, you can proceed with sourcing only.

Yes. We offer two design packages:

  • Standard Design — USD 40/sqm (minimum USD 3,000). Complete residential design including space planning, 3D renderings, material specifications and furniture layout.
  • Premium Design — USD 70/sqm (minimum USD 5,000). For larger residences, high-end materials and complex spaces. Includes VR walkthroughs, detailed millwork shop drawings and priority service.

Both packages include 3 free revision rounds. The design fee is paid in full before we begin the full design process.

As an additional benefit, up to 30% of your design fee can be credited back toward your furniture order when you reach procurement thresholds (10% at RMB 100,000, 30% at RMB 300,000).

For private residential clients, we don't simply search through thousands of factories across China. Instead, we work primarily within a carefully selected supply chain of 30–40 partner factories that our team has personally visited and evaluated.

We focus on the right choices rather than endless choices. Our role is to help you find the right products from the right suppliers, while reducing the risks behind the purchase — quality, materials, workmanship, production lead time, quality control, packaging and delivery.

For B2B clients, we do offer nationwide sourcing across China under a procurement agency fee structure.

Production timelines vary depending on the product type and complexity:

  • Standard furniture (sofas, dining tables, beds): 25–35 days
  • Custom cabinetry / built-in (kitchens, wardrobes): 35–50 days
  • Upholstered items (custom fabric/leather): 30–40 days
  • Lighting & decor: 15–25 days

For a full-home project, we typically coordinate production so all items are ready around the same time, usually within 45–60 days from order confirmation. We'll provide a detailed production schedule after your order is confirmed.

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Bank Transfer (T/T) — Our primary payment method for all orders. 70% production payment before manufacturing, 30% balance payment after QC approval and before shipping.
  • PayPal — Accepted for small payments only (such as the commitment deposit). Please note: PayPal transaction fees are borne by the buyer.

All payments are made to our official company account. We will provide a formal invoice / proforma invoice before each payment.

We take packaging very seriously to minimize transit damage:

  • All items are professionally packed with foam, corner protectors and custom wooden crates for fragile items.
  • We conduct a full quality inspection before packing, and document everything with photos.
  • We recommend purchasing shipping insurance, which we can arrange on your behalf.

If damage does occur:

  1. Document the damage with photos and videos immediately upon receipt.
  2. Notify us within 7 days of delivery.
  3. We will assess the damage and provide a solution — which may include replacement parts, repair guidance, or a credit toward your next order.

For insured shipments, we will assist with the insurance claim process.

We conduct quality control at multiple stages:

  1. Material check — Before production, we verify materials, fabrics, finishes and dimensions match your specifications.
  2. Production follow-up — Our team visits factories during production to monitor progress and quality.
  3. Pre-shipment inspection — Before packing, we conduct a full inspection of every item: dimensions, materials, workmanship, finishes, functionality and packaging.
  4. Packing check — We verify proper packing materials and methods before items leave the factory.

All QC results are documented with photos and shared with you before the balance payment is due. You are also welcome to arrange a third-party inspection or visit the factory yourself.

5. Product Procurement & Payment Terms

5.1 Payment Schedule

  • 70% — Production Deposit: paid after final quotation and order confirmation, before production begins.
  • 30% — Final Balance: paid after quality inspection and client confirmation, before shipment.

The Commitment Deposit is credited toward the 30% final balance. Design fees are paid in full before the full design process begins (see Section 4).

5.2 Quotation Validity

All quotations are valid for 15 days from the date of issue, subject to raw material price fluctuations. Final pricing is confirmed in the signed proforma invoice.

6. Quality Control & Inspection

  • Every order undergoes 100% quality inspection before shipment, covering material check, dimension check, finishing check, and packaging check.
  • We provide quality inspection videos and photos to the client before the final balance is due.
  • Clients may arrange third-party inspection (e.g., SGS, ITS) at their own cost. We will coordinate access to the factory.
  • Production is considered approved once the client confirms the QC report or does not respond within 3 working days of receiving QC media.

7. Lead Times & Delivery

Lead times are estimates and begin after final confirmation of specifications and receipt of the production deposit. Typical lead times are 30–45 working days for standard products and 45–60 working days for custom products, depending on complexity and quantity. CASA TRIO coordinates logistics but is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance, shipping carriers, port congestion, or force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics, war, etc.).

8. Shipping & Logistics

  • Logistics costs are quoted separately after all products are produced and packed. We work with 3–5 stable logistics partners and select the optimal route for your destination.
  • Clients may pay the logistics company directly — CASA TRIO does not mark up shipping costs.
  • We recommend purchasing cargo insurance to minimize loss risk during transit.
  • All export orders use reinforced wooden crate packaging with moisture protection to reduce damage risk.
  • If damage occurs during shipping, we will actively cooperate with the insurance claim process and assist with replacement parts, repair, or refund as applicable.

Clients must confirm all specifications in writing before production begins. Quality inspection videos and photos are provided before shipment.

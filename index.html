04 — How We Work

Three Ways to Work.

For homeowners and businesses. Sourcing scope flexible — furniture, decor, materials, or bulk orders.

All options include Quality Control + Global Delivery + End-to-End Coordination
Option 01

Sourcing Only

Best for: You already have design

You have your own design or drawings — we manage everything end-to-end: sourcing, customization, production oversight, QC and global delivery. You pay us directly, we handle the supply chain.

Get Started
Option 02

Design + Sourcing

Best for: You need design + sourcing

A complete turnkey solution — interior design, 3D visualization, sourcing, production oversight, QC and delivery, all managed end-to-end by us. You pay us directly, we handle everything.

Get Started
Option 03

B2B Sourcing Agent

Best for: Bulk buyers & businesses

For retailers, projects and bulk buyers — we source factories, match supply chains and provide quotations. You pay factories directly, plus a transparent service fee to us. We oversee production, QC and logistics.
